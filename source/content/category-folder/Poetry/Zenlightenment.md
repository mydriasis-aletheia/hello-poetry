Life's a trip
and love is an upper.

The vicissitudinous nature
of fortune's favor
belongs to whosoever dare
grasp their future.

I've had a headache since I woke up
in the year two-thousand-and-sixteen.
There's one thing I've found that makes it
take leave of me; musicality, lyric
and melody, sweet tunes
and strange poetry. 
I must learn to craft
and administer them.

(Become the shamanic cybran,

Embody the aeon philosophy.

I am the Tip of the Spear,
Its haft is this disc-jockey;

I surrender myself to
become thy master of ceremonies.)
