Vitamins (v1)
I said I was going home
but the park was in my way;
Unpredictable is it’s second name.
An assorted crowd was milling about,
So I dropped in to sort something out.
Then by pure chance randomness hit me,
I bought a bounty of yokes
shaped like little ghosties.
Such is the way
of Millennium on a Friday.
I don’t complain, it brightens up my day.

Lets go get crazy.

They taste bitter
so the molly’s fine.
Relief,
The blue ghosts R genuine.
Now the ball’s rolling and I got mine.
Guess what? It’s proving sigma time.
Suspense coming up
then forty-five minutes in
excitement hits
as my brain leaks serotonin.
Love buzz again.

Some gang tried to mug mine,
I laughed in their face
and we walked right by.


Vitamins (v2)
I said I was going home
but unpredictable got in my way.
I wandered into the park, curious, for I saw
a crowd milling about, and curiosity did demand
my time. By pure chance, randomness, I acquired
a bounty of yokes in the likeness of blue ghosts.
Such is the way of Millennium on a Friday.
As quickly I found my mates to show them
the treasure.
We decided
to get dosed.
Bitte.

Suspense coming up, then, forty-five minutes in
excitement hit
as our brains leak serotonin. On love buzz again,
Young demon.
Some gang tried to mug mine, I laughed at them
as we walked by.
Howdy-ho mo-fo?
Nobody could touch
us, I was on fire. Then
drum 'n bass brought everyone higher.

(I'd never've guessed where those vitamins led.
We joked a lot about Breaking Bad. Times change.)
