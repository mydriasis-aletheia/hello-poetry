Curious and most careful, 
I chose responsible dose of 7mg 
where the drug's effect would be both 
apparent and safe (within the dose-range 
of FDA-approved prescriptions, well below 
what would be definitively neurotoxic).

It is potent, stimulation unsubtle
but deceptive in its clear-headedness.
Remarkably efficacious cognitive euphoria,
An unsubtle entactogen, exceptional but,
Its magic marred by apotheogenicity. 
However I found it easy to chat with friends.

Long-lasting, at least 16 hours, with after-effects 
which reduced restfulness (felt inability to enter REM sleep). 
I combined it with 300mg of NAC and some CBD 
in an effort to mitigate anticipated restlessness, but 
this did not yield much benefit. 
Decent experience,
