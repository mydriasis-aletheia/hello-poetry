We went to dinner with some friends
at their new place

and enjoyed the evening.
I find when it's been a while since I've 

been around, gotten out of the house,
I am occasionally 

apart, in others' 
company, but I bought them a peace lily


and it brought me such joy.
Perhaps I am altogether,
A more basic creature
than I had thought;
This consoles me.
