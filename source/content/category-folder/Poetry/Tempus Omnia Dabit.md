 Odds are at some point one of us
may get hurt.

The question is: how will we react?
How will we respond?
Will we be wounded,
Scarred, or will we grow strong?

As long as pain
isn't inflicted upon us, in malice
or negligence, then we should be able to forgive, but

were one of us to act out of spite, malign cruelty, then
it'd be hard to account for intentions
having gone astray, awry, amiss.
Let this never happen to us, and if it does, let there be

wholesome truth and honest reconciliation. I'm in this
for the long haul, and I know we'll do our best
as surely as I know


(Time Will Give All.)
