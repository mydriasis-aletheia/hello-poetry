Should you call someone out
just remember that quality
which you saw in them
also resides within yourself.
Accept this
and remember
it's a two way street.

If you want help all I'm asking
is you be willing to change;
You're not alone, together
we'll get better. Just remember
it's a two way street. Last but not least:
"Help will always be given at Hogwarts 
to those who ask for it".

[Quote:
Line Thirteen and Fourteen offered up by Albus Dumbledore (Richard Harris) in Harry Potter and The Chamber of Secrets]
