Ego death is what I'm searching for,
Śūnyatā, zero-summing;
I am not.

I hope to reverse-engineer Nirvana.
and rather than nothing, become one.

(Comprehension/Trancendension.)


v1
Ego death is what I’m searching for:
Śūnyatā, zero-summing;
I am not.

But with this
the realisation that
because we are not,
I all are we.

Practice ego death.
Reverse-engineer Nirvana.
Then you shall become nothing, and hence all, which is one.

(Comprehension/Trancendension.)
