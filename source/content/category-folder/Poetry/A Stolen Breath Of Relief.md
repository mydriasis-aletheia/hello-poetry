I'm going out tonight, and I'm gonna have a good time.
I hit rock bottom, now I gotta climb, step up to the rungs
and wish the world a kindness I've been searching for,
Then stand here and smile for her.
Want my happiness to be true
and my health never to be at the expense of others'. I want
the world to exhale, and exalt in this release, feel pure relief.


(Expunged.)
