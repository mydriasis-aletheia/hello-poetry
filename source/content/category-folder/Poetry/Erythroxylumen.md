I went for a walk 
with a cup of coca tea
to sooth the burnout, and 

with some music it loosened.

The sun came out, I find myself 
dreaming of home. I wish 
for time, for poetry, music, drugs,

for new ventures, for old scum.

Forget the law, feel life again
for a second. My mind alights
the maze I'm wandering again.
