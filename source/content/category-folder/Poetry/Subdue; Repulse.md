I felt an overwhelming need to get lost
just as school was about to start.
Something was wrong, the sways
had begun to occur for too long.
My mind was not in the right place,
I put it somewhere. Somewhere
away. I spent it. Lost my days; dazed.
Thrown away,
It's all I want now,
To go about obtaining knowledge in all the wrongs ways.

I feel existence laid bare.
My eyes are open, the colors are intact.
Of those who gaze into the abyss,
Few have seen it stare back.

A void, I continued to look at
even when cooler heads' turned
and more courageous hearts' shut
their valves.
It's altered me.
I'd ask a question
that'd bring me back.
Can I perceive what I am?
Who is that?
Is patience the right course of action?
Will it come back?
Am I insane, what of
insanity? Does it slowly
grip me like an ego grows
vanity? Return to the plain from where you absconded?
What might you find
has settled in your mindset?
What see you that'd scares you so?
This psychobabble taken root in my soul.
What eats you,
What do you know?
Are you so scared of what you've grown?

Am I so scared.
What do I know?
What've I become?
Inept, my standards
have fallen, my failures,
Dissatisfaction. Shame. Disgrace.

I already have the answer,
This will make the journey
so much harder. Onward,
There is work to be done.
Before you know it
you'll be back again.

(Craving euphoria.)
