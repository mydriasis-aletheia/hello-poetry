Apathy/lethargy, I do not wish thee 
upon anybody; 
Unmotivated perfection.

***I can't start eternity***.

*Leaves seethe
outside my window*.

I envy
the poor chap
who doesn't know pity.

*I was at
the last supper*.
Lost my train of thought
in a tunnel ***gone dark***.

Stream
of
con-
-cious-
-ness,


(Broken)
