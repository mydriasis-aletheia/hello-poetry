At twenty-six
I wonder what the credits will look like
at the end of my life.


Walking the estate
through my childhood
and adolescence. Nostalgic
loneliness. The awe of discovery
under lamplight. Listen, naked trees
shiver in the chill of winter, touched by
almighty rain-clouds. μ-Ziq plays Goodbye,
Goodbye.

Walking the city
where I grew up and
grow old. Belonging to a
history. I lost myself in study,
To the humanities, and I dabbled
in the dark arts, forbidden knowledge
beyond pharmacy; ineffable wisdom and
unspoken ethics guide me.

(At twenty-six
I wonder what the credits will look like
at the end of my life.)
