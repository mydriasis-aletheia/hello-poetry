I know now
the love I couldn't reach
wasn't out there, it was in my soul.
Ideation without intent of following through,

Want to bring myself to change
but the damage is pervasive.
The highs, the lows, those
teenage years spent lovesick;
As just another drug fanatic
I could escape it, who I am,
Who I learned to love, and
recently, who I learned to
judge. I am so vexed, but
what else is there for me
than my judgements?

Our love, I try
to feel it.

(Where's the love? Where
is all this judgement coming from?
I'm trying to see past it, to see there is more to me.)
