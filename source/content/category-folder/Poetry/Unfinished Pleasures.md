These festive weekends cause such thoughts to stir in me, 
Longing to understand the person, our being here, these notions;
I find it difficult to feel pleasure, too concerned with aponia.

Substance should be used with intention, and we must intend
authentically. I feel I could do so, among them, to trust
and be vulnerable

(in their preasence;)
#B&S23
