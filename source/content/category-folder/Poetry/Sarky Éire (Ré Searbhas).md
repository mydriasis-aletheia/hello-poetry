"G'luck to ya"
sounds like
there's a
Glock
aimed at
your head
but I swear

that's just the
sarcasm talking.

"G'luck to ya" is an Irish abbreviation of "good luck to you" best translated as "that's unlikely" (for whatever the reason) and/or "I'm not with you". It is used to express doubt, disapproval or lack of confidence (given guesstimated chances of success).
