v1
Green and black
apothecary’s wrath,
Justice master,
Who’s got your back?

What’s the difference
between me and you?
Do you like to jump rope?
I’m sure you do.

Que pasa?
Black mamba.
Justice master,
Dance.

Never come down
after sundown.
Twenty four/seven,
Trance

This stereo
goes up to eleven,
Five-thousand watt bass machine.
Phat.

Cruising round Isengard
with The Big Jump blaring,
Mitzi-turbos and ten shpots
for sale but no staring.

Stole a riot shield once
from a guarda station.
Long story short,
It’s kinda crazy.
