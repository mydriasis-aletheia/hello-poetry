I don't know why I feel this way but I know so intimately
what it feels like to be lacking
in the proper amount

of particular neurotransmitters.
Nothing says procrastination
like a big bag of nootropics

on a grey, stormy winter day.

I write because it's the finite detail
with which I can't deal. You know how
afraid I am. Though words may differ
there's a recurring theme;
Killing it, softly. Don't want to write
anymore, don't want people to read it
but I'll still publish it.
People have given me everything and I
just can't be ok. Driving with a concussion.

(Unsound and outta town.)
