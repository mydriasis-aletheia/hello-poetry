I found that ***writing worked best for me***,
Like taking ecstasy, it let me be intimate
not just with others but with myself.

**Ek-stasis**,
Meaning *to stand outside of oneself,
A removal to elsewhere*, for the time being/a while is enough.

Now mark my words, what worked for me ain't necessarily
what will work for ye, after all,
***Some people prefer drawing***.

I find that ***music, dance
or any kind of exercise*** helps but
at the end of the day we're all just looking for what works best.

(While therapeutic doses of MDMA, taken in the correct context, may catalyse the alleviation of symptoms arising from emotional trauma the author does not condone the use of ecstasy, XTC or E. Furthermore, the author states that pills off the street are often dangerous because their contents are unverified.)
