I moved away 
from poetry while 
in pursuit of the law,

But poetry is always nourished 
in the hearts and souls of us all.

The law aspires toward dignity;
Whatever the status of truth, 
It is the art of justice: rule
to honor the good life.


The worry sometime gnaws away at me,
What if I'm just not good enough;
I have to believe in myself,
I have faith in me,
All my parts
as one.
