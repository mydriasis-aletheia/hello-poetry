I am no Lord of The Dissos,
No keeper of the k-hole,
No master of m-space,
No god of the void,
I am merely a student
of those dissociative arts.

In the evening
we titrated our doses
cautiously insufflating DMXE
until its threshold appeared; old hope,
That one could return to the m-verse.
We listened to thumping drumcode
and watched a Soviet adaptation
of The Lord of the Rings. Nonsensical
contexts were generated  by the juxtaposition
of techno with our Russian Frodo and Gandalf the Red.

After two hours of mild dissociation, pleasant warmth
and good company the effect began to gently fade.
I consumed no more than 7mg, it is certainly
worthy of further assessment.

(Perhaps this new generation 
of arylcyclohexylamines 
show some promise)
#Khraniteli 1991
