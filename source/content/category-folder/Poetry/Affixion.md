I don't know that having a stash means I'm an addict.
I trust myself to use or abstain from it responsibly, but
I entertain the possibility I'm still an addict just to be safe.
Addiction is a mindset.

I forgot I am a criminal.
Possession of psychoactive substances is too normal to me.
I am committed to understanding mind through the lens
of pharmacophenomenology.

I obtained and used psychoactives in many contexts.
I acknowledge the legal and social consequences
of my actions, how this might affect my life.
I make a point of my behavior, ethical integrity, mental health.

Of course, I want to believe I am not an addict
but isn't addiction when a pattern of behavior
adversely affects one's life? 

Withdrawal effects can be hidden, and
they only indicate physical dependency
which is distinct from addiction.

Addiction is a moral judgement.
Morality is premised on a conception of
'the good life'. In this case I wonder, am I only an addict

when I am caught? No,
Not while I judge myself.
