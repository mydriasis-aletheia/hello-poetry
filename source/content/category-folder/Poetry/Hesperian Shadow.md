Back from the county town, my past lives
collide with my present course, I'm tinged 
with nostalgia, memories 
of my upbringing; coming-of-age, young adulthood;
Consequence, aftermath.

I can't shake the place I was born and reared,
A town so submetropolitan.

Back across from way upstate, I am with 
thoughts of relatives I hardly knew, and 
an alternate life that never was
under the flag of the United States; daydreams
and wondering; whoever I'd have been.

I can't shake the cultures I was brought up with,
A healthy moon over a rose so paracelsian.
