What sky?
In half-light
this umbra moves,
‘Tis not the sun we're waiting for
but the moon!

Ecliptic: darkstar, blacksun; moonlight.
Amp up the equinox/Tryp through the solstice.
We'll make it through to this apocalypse.

Yearn for a disclosure of knowledge.
