About two years ago
I lost the two things
I had held closest,
What hurt most
was that nobody told us.
It went on for months.
More than a year
should have been enough
time for this to heal.
Alas, there is a gap in my life
and a hole in my heart.
My bandmate took my girl
while the other one stood silent.
***Maybe that's karma,***
Albeit a very severe dose of it.
These two guys were my brothers,
She was my lover
and I am a fool
if I still suffer.
I think about this less than often
but there is pain there, throbbing.
While I may have my regrets,
*It was worth it.*

After this I went into a reckless little tailspin,
My take on life changed something fierce:
I felt there was nothing to lose,
Hence I could only win.
Then, a discovery, the phenomena of dealing.
Society openly scorned it, the law totally abhorred it,
Few understood it, fewer took to it.
All I knew was for once people appriciated me
and with that I was hooked instantly.
How could I not be?
It was my only relief,
Pretending to be someone who wasn't myself.
Intoxication became the only thing I had to live for;
I'd lie in wait for the weekend to land.

I changed so much and there's no going back.
Not that I can, or could, or would;
***It gave purpose to me.***
Might as well be unrepentant,
After all it's all I've got
and nothing should last as long.

I deem myself to be a depressed fiend
though I doubt that has any real meaning
(with or without, *insert sarcastic air quotes*,
**"The drugs"**).

*"Love, love, love.
What is it good for?
Absolutely nothing."*

I still get numb but I don't think they know,
I learnt to hide that void long ago.
Here's a question that really bothers me:
If one is in agonizing emotional pain
but it doesn't affect it's performance
as a functioning member of society
why should anybody help? Say there is no suicide risk
and that's a certainty (For he grappled with it long before
and in that grapple did uncover the wor(l)d).
I think I'm depressed but that counts for nothing,
I would refuse anti-Ds if ever given the option.
Well what say ye?
I am lonely
and feel no urge to not to be.
I doubt I'd be labelled good
by conventional ethics (though perhaps
morality might do me some justice).
I can only guess at how I am perceived,
I might yet appear to have something going for me; **Lies.**
With no real want nor any true need
why should I ever intend to leave
this beatific narcotic quagmire behind me.

I think you should know though:
While I may one day *forgive you*
I'm never going back.
The bridges I burn
will light the way forward
and incinerate what came to pass.

I was too tired to think of a name for this story,
Yes, I know; I write abysmally love-sick
and abnormally drug-obsessed prose/poetry.

(Quote:
Lines Forty-Eight, Forty-Nine and Fifty - Effy Stonem, Skins S2E7)
