***Exhortation:***
*Greetings,*
Let no one hesitate to write poetry while young, and let no one tire of it when old, for it is never too soon nor too late to devote oneself to the well-being of the soul.
The act of self-expression lays bare our rumination; true poetry is written with only one reader in mind: the self and the divine.
We press out our emotions and it feels right, to stand outside of oneself and view that being as a manifestation of change: time.

***Don�t Fear Society***
Understand that **the judgement we assert provides us with a chance to understand oneself**, think about that whenever a remark is made, be it by yourself or on your part; it is a reflection of what they have seen not merely what you have heard.
Events are always moving through a being's consciousness at varying levels of awareness, by examining their place in that individual we better comprehend them, and therefore, us.
I encourage you to play with words for they are the basic tools of communication between minds, the glass through which we see each other and a mirror for the sublime.

***Don�t Fear Insanity***
**Sanity can be measured in our ability to move on** from something that troubles us, only obsessive repetition allows anything to bother us, be it from behaviour or thought; we need only ask of ourselves what have we done to warrant a rethinking of our action. To question whether or not one is sane is to fundamentally question our control over reality as we perceive it. After all, one who is immersed in psychosis assumes the validity of their reality. Therefore simply questioning your sanity immediately assures you of its recovery, even if you must first establish what recursive patterns' haunt it. 

To fall prey to madness (momentary or otherwise) isn't uncommon in this day and age yet this is not the mark of a healthy brain, it belays some other ailment; be it physiological or psychosomatic, whether it is rooted in the genetic or memetic is difficult to establish: nature versus nurture, the debate continues!

***We are***  **only human**, prone to error, we must accept our limitations yet endeavour to prosper. All we need do is ask the question: to what end [*Apotheotelos*]; the only exception being things we cannot (insofar) reasonably comprehend [*Entheogenesis*] (though curiosity and scepticism are in-themselves a worthy end).
***The beauty of a teleology
tempered by the assessment of reality.***

Most people would claim insanity a fate worse than death, it is neither worse nor better for **one cannot realize they are insane any more than realize they have died for the very act of revelation defeats the claim.** One who is wise neither accepts sanity nor fears a lack of it, they simply enjoy every moment of it, as is, while questioning.
Repetition does not offend nor does it entail any kind of suffering, in fact habit bestows some of the greatest pleasures that can be found but we must always ask of ourselves how and why?

*The human difference engine is yourself, the person who has the courage to act as the person they'd want to see in everybody, true to an ideal.*

***Master your understanding***
**In observation we open ourselves up to understanding,** creating mirror neurons which recreate event-data within our own schema; this is the essence of The Empatheon, seeking comprehension, to stand both in and outside of the ego is to superposition it, juxtaposed with reason.
A clear recognition of ignorance enables one to admit when argument is fruitless and choice is opinionated; an honest connection to others allows one to share in being with them.

As important is an awareness of one's self and the ability to relinquish your ego's help, the wisdom of when to be selfless; but overall
***know how to be alone and take pleasure in it,***
Abstain from company when necessary but do it graciously.

**Everything we do is an evolution of patterns in motion.**
These physical patterns constitute every moment of waking consciousness as mental frames attack/sustain/decay/fade.
Each moment is refracted as any memory is reflected,
***Only in determining does one truly occupy the present.***

*Conclusion:*
***Consider these thoughts by day and at night.*** Discern them on your own or discuss them as you wish (preferably in the company of like-minded friends). **Do not deceive yourself,
Remember who you are, you're alive; and you shall live godlike amongst humankind,
'cause one whose life is fortified by their own true spiritual expression in no way resembles a mortal being.**

(Exhortation inspired by Epicurus's "Letter to Menoeceus")
