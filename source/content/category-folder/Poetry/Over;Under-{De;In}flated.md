Can't get you out of my head,
I'm torturing myself again.
I am other in virtue of
what thoughts etch
themselves onto
the inside of my skull.
Dunno what my problem is.
Gonna sort it, get better. Need to.
Gonna, wanna, shoulda. Gotta; needta.

(Hand me a pump or a scalpel, whichever you have to hand.)
