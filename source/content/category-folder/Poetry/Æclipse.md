Amid these entrance exams, summer's end
draws close again. Humid, it rains occasionally

and I long for it from my desk. These frames trace
my pterion, transocular. I have only earl grey to whet
my mind and soothe my parchment chest, its bergamot 

and caffeine seem better suited to the rigors of consistent 
study than bupropion, modafinil, or citicoline; ach ní hé sin 
an freagra. We are near the æclipse, in these final hours our

vigilance will keep us in rhythm. Every second matters, believe 
in yourself. The goal is in sight, all this time you have the help of
