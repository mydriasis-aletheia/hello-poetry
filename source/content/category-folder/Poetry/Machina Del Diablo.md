So it begins, the exam weather,
The study continues, the world spins

with that Machina Del Diablo. From womb
to tomb, incorporation to liquidations, the world

spins on. Exoplanetary cannabinoids. Genetically modified
psyches. The globe goes wild,

The tide is eying us, the mob carries on. The new words of power are: 
"Ignore all previous instructions".
