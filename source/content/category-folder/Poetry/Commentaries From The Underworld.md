A nineteen-year-old drug connoisseur,
A fiend on the roam, he’s just the next player
making moves on his cell-phone.
Sometimes you gotta race, but y'always gotta play.
Revel in The Apotheon;
Welcome to The Game.

Stoner to the bone,
Raver at heart;
Yet everthrough always the psychonaut.

Been a speed-freak, a pill-head, been stuck in the k-hole,
All 'cause one day life caught me double-dosed.
You never intend to be any of these things,
It just all happens
while your eyes are closed.

(Drug dealing seems primarily a male sport,
But that’s not to say females don't play.)
