Broke my hand cycling away from something
in my psyche. Felt nothing,
Earth-grazer. Rush of
adrenaline; tumbling, I fall
and my anger turned inward. Harm unto me.

They gave me a pair of local anesthetics
for the surgery, not psychoactive although,
the level of physical detachment from my limb
was curious. The nerve-block employed lidocaine
and bupivacaine, the latter was mixed
with epinephrine to increase its duration of action
and this resulted in shivering and anxiety.

(I suppose it is
the archetypal stimulant.)
