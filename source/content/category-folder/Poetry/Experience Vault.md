My backlog of trip reports is old, it overflows
and so follows my own psychoactive history,
A summary of my whole experience vault:

I started in 2012, a mere 17
and fortunate enough to have found a crowd about town.
We spent our time hanging the park, the square.
We planned to go camping one weekend,
My friend's brother offered me a pill;
Curious, I paid for a half
and that evening ate half a blue Einstein. 
It was a memorable, I felt accepted,
Safe, among friends, with no pressure to be anyone 
other than myself. It let me feel the context more,
These people, that sense of euphoria.
(-"Reclamation Proclamation".)
Schoolwork took precedence for the rest of the year.

During the summer of 2013 I roamed the town
and like newly weds we embarked
upon our XTC honeymoon, smitten
with the pills of our day: blue ghosts and stars;
Our eyes mydriatic with MDMA
("Proving Sigma", "Full Sigma").

Towards the end of that summer 
a friend came upon a few tabs of acid,
Hofmanns containing LSD. Ever curious
I took a quarter of a stamp, quite a low dose 
but enough to laugh a lot. Later we trekked back
to a friends house, and I watched Dazed and Confused
for the first time. It was lit.

By the end of this year I had taken my first steps
into a wider world, a world of psychonautics,
And on the last day I took one giant leap.

New Years 2014, a night like no other
which I can neither forget nor remember.
I obtained two grams of amphetamine at a rave,
Accidentally did a bump of ketamine (unexpectedly),
I took a couple pills, and smoked a great deal of hashish.
Darkly hedonic, a classic instance of drug misuse.
I recall a point where I took so much
pink champagne speed-amphetamine
that saw these brilliant golden lightning CEVs
(which I've only ever seen replicated in Scorsese's Wolf 
of Wall Street). For weeks afterward I was extremely burnt out. 
("Mr. Nobody", among others.)
College work took precedence for the next couple months.

The second time I encountered LSD was early Spring of 2014,
I acquired a ten-strip from a dread-locked crusty,
The tabs were practically dripping they were as fresh
Shiva prints, probably 100µg laid by dropper.
I went tripping with people I hardly knew 
and of course I had a frightful time. 
I felt my mind squirm in their company,
It was overwhelming - unfamiliar set and setting.
As a rule one should only trip among friends.
One moment perhaps made it worthwhile,
As I was listening to Your Loft, My Acid
by Death in Vegas, I closed my eyes
and saw galaxies moving, spirals spinning
as stars glimmered; the Hubble Ultra-Deep Field
but I only realized this when I came across that picture
years later.

I found my people in college, kindred spirits
with much to appreciate in respect to temperament
and intelligence. There were societies, clubs, pubs, parties;
Crews, crowds, households and gatherings. 
Through one group I was introduced to house/techno night
in its brief infancy, and unfortunately one night 
I ate a bad pill which contained 100mg of PMA (a green apple). 
As the effects began to manifest I felt ill, cold and sweaty.
I could feel the neurotoxicity, my brain groaned scarily.
This was a terrible time in my life, difficult for a few reasons.
I like to think I changed for the better. 
My philosophy of pure hedonism began to wane,
I leaned towards a more Epicurean outlook.

Despite the turmoil of my last trip I would drop acid again,
This time on a warm summer day in 2014 with closer friends.
The tabs were Jim Morrisons containing 175-180µg of LSD.
We soared through the chasms of our minds
but remained jovial, splitting our sides
with laughter at the most inane things,
Like listening to 'Wiggle' by Jason Derulo (appalling when sober).
This was my first ++++ trip [Shulgin Rating Scale].
The visual and cognitive effects of the drug were fully manifest.
I experienced extraordinary motion permeate my visual field
and extend into the imagination.  I had thought processes
I can only describe as semiotic insofar as they confronted me
with the purported connections of language and world.
In this regard I was lucky enough to experience
what PsychonautWiki's refers to as level 8A geometry:
"Perceived exposure to the semantic concept network".
I am grateful for having experienced this and think it
a phenomenal indication of the importance of psychedelics
as they pertain to the study of human consciousness.
I became passionate about understanding how we go about
experiencing things, and this remains a lifelong passion.

Duing that summer I lived for the sesh
and eventually became well-acquainted
with the various recreational compounds
and sought more exotic and rare material.
It felt more wholesome then, though I was
still green (at heart). It's been so long since
these experiences the details are faded
but I try to remember as I know many more
are to be forgotten forever.

Mephedrone was popular around then
and I acquired a few red Route 66 pills
containg 150mg of the stuff, 4-MMC.
I took one and saw The National play [GIAF]
with a friend. It was enjoyable, though I do remember
the oddness of coming down alone in my house.
I found it be decent material when used responsibly.
People were generally apprehensive of mephedrone,
It was then still considered a research chemical [RC].
An inept ecstasy dealer went so far as to return one pill to me which I thought he'd appreciate. He was too scared to take it.
When faced with a choice between the old reliable
and a new proof, we see the user's rationale.
I recall another evening about the town
when one of my close friends
found a bag of the stuff
in the smoking area of our usual.
We passed around the 'plant food' (a name
harking back to head-shop days). I'm glad some pictures
from that night (among others) exist, they were such times.
One of my few regrets is not taking more photographs.
I reckon 4-MMC is probably the best of the 'drone analogues,
It's the OG cathinone.

On another occasion we went camping by Menlo Castle.
for a lad's birthday. Someone I knew gifted me some
home-synthed MDA which was weak enough
and had that 'fluttery' character about it.
There was also MDMA present that night but
I didn't indulge and got a taxi at 4am. I had to pick up
my friend's guitar amp the next morning as he lent it to them
for the night. They were still coming down when I wandered
into the flat they ended up in. The atmosphere in there
was one of such palpable depression it literally
sucked the energy out of you when you entered.
No doubt the cumulative result of frequent/excessive MDxx use.

There is a downside to drug use
and it is worth thinking it through.
I don't think that should deter a healthy individual
with a curious mind. Best to be aware
of the negative implications of drug use,
However, drug abuse is a different beast
with a real dark side.
In matters of recreations sometimes the lines become blurred.
Most people toe the line at some point,
Often it;s with alcohol (given its place in Western society) but
you see it with caffiene, nicotine, cannabis.
I've written about 'apotheogens' (i.e. addictive/subversive substances
such as opioids, benzodiazipines, cocaine and methamphetamine),
but any substance can be used responsibly or improperly.
Even those we consider non-addictive or self-regulatory.
can be apotheosized. I used psychedelics in such fashion
at points during that summer. I recall acid binges and
candy-flipping across the town, it was not wholesome.
I was inexperienced, the underlying theme was of escape
and I am sure we struck an image, waste-fellows all,
Bandying about, bouncing around until it was time for us
to sneak home. Enough of that, there are more worthwhile tales
of better and stranger trips than plain old acid.

I recall when we took DOC, a psychedelic amphetamine.
I split a couple tabs between a good few college friends
and we wandered about the campus for hours on end.
The effect of its powerful mental stimulation
was overwhelming at times, as well as its strong body high,
But anything's manageable with decent people.
Although, I do remember two them debating
about if it was possible to cleanly piss over
one's own shoulder (odd arguments ensued).
Eventually the effects faded, it seemed like eternity.

I should give some detail to those many hazy days
spent smoking on campus. We'd hang out in The Hub,
Our living room away from home. As we were of the city
when the holidays came we were often the only people
to be seen or heard in Áras na Mac Léinn. Many days
were spent listening to music in/from The View, or
watching GoT on the projectors in empty classrooms
or mucking about in the old Acoustic Rooms.
Often we'd walk down by the river and smoke a joint
or go to any of a million other spots. Those days were the best,
Those endless days of summer,
And the company kept, you wish
it would last forever.

It's worth mentioning I attended my first festival [Body & Soul 2014],
which takes place on the weekend of the summer solstice in June.
So much happened that weekend, but those details live elsewhere.
I will say on the Sunday I tried 2C-B for the first time, it was absurd
and I listened to Caribou (Dan Snaith) on repeat
for the next month; Swim (2010).

I should think it just prior to September
when we split some dual and trinity tabs
of NBOMe at a friend's cabin. One contained 750µg of 25i & 25c,
And another contained 700µg of 25i & 300µg of 25c & 25b.
Both types of tab were complexed with HPBCD
to increase bioavailibility. At the time
NBOMe was a more recent addition to the scene.
As a psyche it is unique in its high potency, affinity, and selectivity
for binding to the 5-HT2A receptors. It is considered
to be less safe than other psychedelics. Nevertheless
we had a grand old time as night fell, coming up by Lough Atalia
and merrily smoking weed in an empty playground.
Later we meandered down to our campus and ended up
all sprawled into an alcove under the concourse, joyously chatting,
Smoking, laughing. Above us a sign aptly read: 'Chemical Department'.

I learned much that summer, about my city and of my mind,
I felt so alive. Seasons pass, and another academic year begins
along with another set of experiences.
In late October of 2014 we tried moxy (5-MeO-MiPT)
at a rather high dose, between 10-14mg. We marched from our
pre-game spot on campus [the LAR] into town so we could dance
to D'n'B/Neurofunk [at Puzzle]. During this gig
I experienced synesthesia, quite literally feeling sound wrap
around me as I spontaneously moved to its beat.
Exceptional emotional content to the experience,
Sparse visuals, it was very much in the body.
The first half of the trip is more like a stimulant
entactogen/empathogen, then the second half shifts
into a more conventionally psychedelic trip.
I have much to say on its virtues
and potential uses.

During November I went to a party with one dose of GHB
and found it to be most enjoyable. Its subjective effects
are between those of alcohol and ecstasy. I did
experience a rather unpleasant offset while walking home,
During which I felt my cognitive ability was impaired.
It is probably too apotheogenic, a hard drug
too closely related to alcohol (yet feels serotonergic).
For whatever reason I distrust GABArgics, they're appealing
and their aftereffects are as disconcerting.

It could have been December
when a few of us tried miprocin (4-HO-MiPT).
I'd consulted PsychonautWiki and dosed accordingly, between
20-22mg. We began the evening in fairly standard fashion,
Pre-drinking in The View, then out to a gig [Puzzle].
When the effects came on we were dancing away, but
they were strong, so strong I was sure I had misdosed us.
After enough confusion we needed to get out, we wandered
up to a hill overlooking The Stacks and took a moment
before heading to a mate's gaf. What surprised me was not just
the strength of the trip but its distinct embodied component,
I now think it is characteristic of MiPT derivatives (having tried
a few others which also have a tendency to promote 'body-trips'). We did learn to appreciate this component of the high
as the night wore on. I remember one moment clearly
where my friend held the hood of his jacket by the back of his hand
so that the shadows cast by his jacket created a hypnotic silhouette
on the wall, and as he moved in time with the music it danced, its
movements smooth yet intricate, and its appearance
animate with odd intention.

By the end of 2014
my voyage into psychonautics was well underway.
Whether consciously
or not, I was becoming an acolyte in a worldwide cabal.
The RC scene was so exciting, we were at the forefront of psychoactive technology. It was invigorating but required
one be knowledgeable enough to navigate it safely.
This was a reason to learn more neuroscience,
If I could understand a drug of one class
and how it's mechanism interact
with others' then I understand the dangers
associated and can attempt to extrapolate them to
their respective chemical analogs.
Further to this, there are noteworthy psychological phenomena
to be aware of. Participating in psychoactive rituals
can modulate the bonds we share with each other.
It is tempting to think
this extends beyond psychology and sociology
for friendships are extraordinary things
which I reckon we take for granted.
I digress, it is hard to know
the exact time of every event.
I used photographs to pin down
dates but fewer photos exist of these
times, and for good reason. We were
criminals for pursuing self-discovery,
And photos were potential evidence.
I deeply regret this, it is so simple
but it would give me such comfort
to have another thing to remember
these years by. That is why I write now,
To preserve these lost parts of our lives.
I am no less afraid of LE, but I am sure
that this is my past and not who I am.
It's not like all I did was take illegal drugs,
This is a trip report, and I guess also a story.
In contrast to the last year, New Years was more human.
Many groups came together under one roof, there was
a great deal of alcohol and a few spliffs. It was a sight
to see such different crowds together, and I'm sure
I had to manage more than my fair share of drama.

I am afraid 2015 is patchy. I continued my studies
and thrived on the university atmosphere, I recall
a couple parties: I spent much time preparing
or dispensing the psychoactive of the day
with due attention given to the recipient.
My belief in the value of the experience
motivated me, gave a sense of purpose.
Any gains were reinvested into material
that'd propel the cause. I was small-time,
I only wanted to explore this strange world,
To help others in their own quest to get better
and be with them for a time, for it to be alright.
That year I saw two sides of the session
and reconsidered the place of MDMA,
Its suitability as a party drug which
seemed to contradict its value as
a potential medicine. I've seen
many people misuse, abuse
and profit off it, and have
done so myself. This is the way it will be
as long as it remains scheduled substance.

In February I believe I went to a music event [TOME]
and took 2C-B. I found it quite difficult, the music was stale,
Repetitive without the possibility of meaning. The crowd
was mostly on MDxx but the atmosphere was fatigue.
In popularity it was pushing past its prime and
dragging everyone here down with it, they
were running on fumes, having burnt
out; a sign of ecstasy's dark side.
I did not enjoy tripping here.

Some time I should think was in the winter, I took MDMA
with a few other college mates. I have a memory of listening
to Flume's Tennis Courts remix as we peaked.
Their joy and wonder was what I craved but
it was the psychologically restorative aspect
which I believed in. Seeing this associated with the afterglow
assured me I was doing good. To bring people this experience
which had healed me, and to extend that to others, for people
to be able to speak openly of their pain and thereby process
the emotion they had been holding. In this way they feel
how they are not damaged and can move on with their lives.
In this way I sought to improve the world, one person at a time.
I thought this the ethical way to use MDMA, its light side.

On another occasion someone greedily took a bit too much.
He was fine but when asked of my dose I replied 'one pill
is enough'; I'd amend that now as it's often too much.

I can think of one trip in March which went awry.
Nothing particularly bad, just on edge due to
an obvious misjudgment of setting. Never
go tripping on Saint Patrick's Day. Get drunk
or smoke the green. I ate 20mg of MAL at 3.15pm
and this is considered low dose but it came on strong.
I remember playing Cards Against Humanity in The Hub
while coming up. There was some visual activity,
Much mental stimulation, and a bit of temperature dysregulation.
Later that evening I ended up in a house near the docks
where people were tripping on moxy. It's worth noting
that the after-effects from MAL lasted maybe 48 hours.

A few trips at a friend's house come to mind but no exact dates.
It must have been during the summer. We did 4-AcO-xxT
a few times, usually 20mg, but of more interest are those
less common and more exotic research chemical.

One of these was DPT, myself and a mate took 125mg orally
which was wasteful because of its poor bioavailibility.
Some indication of the general vibration though:
Unlike DMT (the benchmark for base tryptamines)
it last between 2-4 hours and has seemingly slower visuals.
(Trying to describe this makes me wonder if it isn't possible
to measure the frequency at which psychedelics interrupt
consciousness.) I would say its afterglow is quite pronounced.

On another occasion we each ate just over 100mg of βk-2C-B
which I found quite interesting (and another quite unpleasant).
Interesting to have a psychedelic cathinone if little else,
Comparatively (to the phenethylamine) its visual component
could be described as a tad more speedy, there were differences
in its body-high (but the details are lost to me).

One final trip I can remember from this time is Methoxetamine,
A longer-lasting and more potent dissociative than Ketamine
which I had previously tried at 25mg (without clear effect).
We all jumped in at 50mg and it was just grand,
It inspired "Aeon Dissociative and The M-Verse".
I think the compound is to be praised for being
less physically harmful (due to increased potency)
and subjectively warmer than K (due to some serotonergicity). When used properly it is unparalleled in its efficiency/effects.
It is near impossible to find nowadays
due to various bans, a real shame.

I recall an occasion when we dropped acid
at a friend's free house. The tabs were Twins,
150µg of exceptional LSD laid via needlepoint.
Very clean. I remember the clock vibrating,
Iit's circular frame pulsing, contorting
like Salvador Dali's painting:
The Persistence of Memory. 
I recall standing out the back, smoking
as dawn broke and spending the next day
on the couch together, watching the first season
of Suits. Really great weekend, had to include it.

I should mention I returned to my first festival [Body & Soul],
This time bringing my own crew (and again, plenty of Buckfast).
Again, the details are available elsewhere so I'll be brief, but
I must mention most of the group dropped 5mg tabs of DOM
on the Saturday. I remember lying on my back staring at the sky
and watching molecules assemble on its wide open azure canvass.

Another occasion well worth relating
is a quiet night in with Mescaline HCl.
One of the greatest psychoactive compounds found in nature,
It was difficult to acquire. We split a gram three ways,
Consuming 330mg each. Marvelous body-effect
which continues to inform my subsequent 2C-x experiences.
I found the comedown quite noticeable at a point,
I think due to it being a more embodied trip.
A must for any psychedelic connoisseur.

I recall a night I combined 4-HO-MET with Cannabis
and saw clearly a lazy tracer scrawl itself across my wall.
Strange euphoria indeed. It was that trip in which I became
quite fond of cous-cous as a palatable snack.

There is one trip I have written about before
where I came to a strange Door in my consciousness while
on a combination of 4-AcO-MET, 4-HO-MET, and Cannabis.
It was unquestionably a ++++ experience, I wrote "Imagine
{A Quiet Night In, Tripping[slash]Break On Through} Emptiness"
to clarify this experience. I think here
is a good place to end these recounts.
I left the country to study abroad
shortly thereafter, returning
after a year. I change in that time,
Reading me I think you'll see that.
I must say there are distinct points
in one's philosophy when you reach for new values;
But here I feel we came across something Other as we
went further in, our use obtained a spiritual dimension.
This became a boon to our emotional health. Moreover,
Many of these experiences bestow intuitions
on the nature of things. I feel as if
I was at times given an emic view into belief
and in this way its formative elements.
I went on to study such things.

It seems obvious to me that the properties of hallucinogens
make them incalculably useful to the philosopher.
I do not consider psychonautics as 'research', instead
it is a practice which can lead to insight
on matters of self and mind.
I must note that the times I have covered
my goals were always partly recreational.
This is something I later forsake
in striving to study these things
with scholarly disinterest.
I am not entirely sure if that is the right way
or if such disinterest is possible
but I tried it for the sake of knowledge.
Regarding my past hedonism, please do not
mistake me for these writings, memories I had
are not events I endorse. My only intention now
is to study and better understand the phenomenon,
To bring to the table my philosophy of psychoactivity.

I have brushed over the years which
marked my entry into psychonautics.
I now leave the contents of this vault


(behind, so that others might read of my experiences
and gain something from them as I have.)
