Get in touch with your inner-demon.

I found mine,
He's not a bad guy,
He showed me the ropes;
Tethered to the sky.

*Atlas endures.*

Incite debauchery
in the heavens above,
Invoke music,
Provoke love.

*Atlantean allure.*

Chained to the sky,
Bound to the air,
Break perception,
Open a tear.

*Neo-classical heir.*

Skybound to the future,
The past under the sea,
The present forlorn holds the key.

(Scions of Atlas
chartered the globe.)
