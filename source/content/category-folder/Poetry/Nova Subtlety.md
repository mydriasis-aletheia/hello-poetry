I ripped out my eyes
so I could see sound,
Then stalked the earth
as it spun ever-'round

The sky turned red
with the blood of stars,
Chthonic heat
melted tar.
Underworld denizens
fled the scene,
For the storm had swept
Tartarus clean.
A hellion riot 
was underway,
And angelic anarchy 
had it's day.

No acumen to be found
in those novae eyes,
No subtlety,
No disguise.
