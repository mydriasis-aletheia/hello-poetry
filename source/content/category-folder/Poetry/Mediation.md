There's always time to re-examine, questions that are always advancing.
To sell drugs, to be aware of the danger, to acknowledge it. These things;
The morality, the ethicality and the responsiblity for all thy would distribute.
Why? Justify. How? To be expected to explain what foolishness
could provoke such highly stigmatized crimes against the state?
When my time cometh in the court of law
there is nothing I can say, naught at all, for to speak my mind
will surely be my downfall. I cry to think when words will be lost;
So here they are, it's all I've got:

***If you're gonna deal then do it responsibly.***
They are only so many people who care.
Wherein the psychoanalytical profile
of distribution comes to bear.
Examine human nature.

Forbidding ensures it shall be done,
Converse thinking suggests gain in it for someone,
This way of conclusion holds nothing and alas there is none.
If only science had prevailed
perhaps the war on drugs would be fought under the microscope.

Now the research is left to those who know not
what is imbued in many of the substances they use.
We forget the humane applications
of our grand molecular tools.
They have been lost to the streets,
They have become our playthings; soiled by commodity, abandoned to recreation.
I'm sad to say what I once loved has changed it's ways.

When I relinquish these powerful forces
they will be told; It is 
all I can do, to explain.
The how is the key, only then might there be why
and true application might we find. (Finality is in their eyes.)

Who else will be responsible
when truth and liberty have been outlawed
and the lives of friends are caught in the crossfire.

You ignore the dilemma, I elect myself the mediator.
