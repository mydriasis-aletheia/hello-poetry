Perhaps they no longer excite me as they used to
but I am still fascinated by the compounds.
It is a life-long passion of mine
to understand and document psychoactivity.
I attempted to cultivate some objectivity towards
these things I've taken to be objects of study.
Perhaps I forgot
consciousness as a process,
And perhaps I should read more

(A. N. Whitehead.)
