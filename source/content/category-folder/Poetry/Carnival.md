In a post-apocalyptic world free
of consumptive communism
or carnal capitalism
people will be free to choose what they learn.
Former socio-indulgence
in the suppression of heart
will be replaced by the knowledge
that life is art.

But will we remember that
somebody ripped down the canvas long ago
because they couldn't share the paint,
Before the atom's cloud distorted our faith.

Now that we're over the under
there's no going back.

Cause we love to riot.
