Amidst traipsing
through the town
I had such a clarity
as to where my soul
had been, as to where
I had gone;
***Nu kyr'adyc,
Shi taab'echaaj'la***.
Who is it that's survived me?
Amongst my menacings
about this city
I was

*human, briefly*

(but now you feast
upon me

and I wonder,
Am I not a machine?
Are we not all proponents

of our own advocacies
or do some of us
have underlying cause
only you can see;
Intentionality?)
