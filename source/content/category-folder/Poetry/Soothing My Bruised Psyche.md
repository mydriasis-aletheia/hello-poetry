I feel ashamed for having had feelings
for another man. He had mental health
issues as well. I did empathize, perhaps
a bit too keenly but I didn't say anything
because of the shame. I am not gay nor do
I consider myself as bisexual, heteroflexible
is probably a better descriptor if we're going
to be labelling. Am I confused? Yes, definitely.
Why I feel ashamed is what I've been querying,
If I'm queer I'll own that one but why I feel such
fear at the prospect of admitting it to anyone has
confounded me. I am afraid of the labels, I've had
enough of that, I do not want another one attached.
I suppressed this for so long out of fear, emotionally
it stunted me but how long can I run from the mirror?
It feels right writing this and perhaps in time I will feel
confident enough say it aloud and admit to those I trust,
If need be, given the time. I will not feel shame any longer,
It is not wrong nor bad nor even uncommon and I am a man.
Why I felt shame I understand but the reasons demand answers
and the answers are bad, they speak ill of my society and plausibly
my upbringing will come into question. The former I could critique
but it is the latter wherein lies the difficulty. There are some things I'd rather not have to remember but we swore never to forget and I'll not willingly break our word nor will I succumb to regret. Thanks A,
Though you'll never know it, I think you helped me
to realize which is always key, I wish it could have been
but we had our share of issues and I wasn't able at the time
so now I thank a ghost in my memory
and ask myself, nay, plead with me
to allow myself the chance to heal.

I will accept all that I am owed if
you let me feel again.

So go on
and just
heal me.


(I have to
think I'm ready
to repair my battered,
Bruised and broken psyche.

People tormented me but no
longer will their ghosts chain my soul.)
