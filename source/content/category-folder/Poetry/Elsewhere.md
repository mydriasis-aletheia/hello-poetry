I want to believe
I do not use drugs
for the traditional
reasons. This entails
crafting a morality
and there is nothing
as perilous; good, bad
and evil. I hold
psychoactive substances
to be inanimate.
At this moment I
am not sure I can
distinguish my use
from others,
But now I will write
otherwise.

I realized in the shower

my reason for continuing down
the path. I'm sure it's
clear that it's not
for recreational or spiritual
purposes. Neither
theraputic notions
nor addictive patterns
motivate the territories
I meander through.

My interpretation is
I'm pushing the limits
of what I feel as human.
I am willing
to test
the condition of humanity,
The threshold of an animal,
The boundary of those things.
I am willing to lose my mind
while I search for something Else;

(And I would juxtapose that
with an understanding of Other.)
