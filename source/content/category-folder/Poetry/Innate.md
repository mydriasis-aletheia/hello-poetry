Born to say “fuck it!”

Cleanse it all,
Wash away the innocence.

Went tripping with alchemy,
The river started shivering.

“In the land of gods and monsters,
I was an angel.
Living in a garden of evil.”

The eternal party rages on,
We could never subsist.
Driven by that haunting youthen bliss.

Wake me before I die,
It’s only gonna happen one time.
I don’t want to miss anything.
