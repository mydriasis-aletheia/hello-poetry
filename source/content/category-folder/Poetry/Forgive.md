I coaxed my mind into a slightly manic dissociative state
and almost broke with narcotic craving.
I really want to get high

but I got no one to share
all my poison arrows and toxic darts with.
I wait until this state fades before returning to my study

of pharmaceuticals, research chemicals, and designer drugs.
Searching for knowledge in all the wrong places,
These psychoactive acquaintances.

Later that week I took a threshold 5mg dose of αMT
and experienced slight color enhancement
and mood elevation. The day after
I ate half a pellet of flubromazepam
and found it enjoyable if unremarkable.
The day after that I drank 15mg of 4C-D in 
water and there's something decent there but
its character was muted by the benzo's residuals. 
The day thereafter I had 3.5mg of 5-MeO-DiPT, which
was a little less coherent at the tail end
but otherwise fine. With potentially limitless 
new psychoactives 

I sometimes wonder
why keep score 
at all.

There's some good in it: curiosity and pleasure 
are a means-in-themselves 
but must be sought responsibly. After all things,

The chemicals are only as good as their company.

(Forgive me if I've been impatient.)
