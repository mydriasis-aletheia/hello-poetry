"Everybody wants to rule the world" but
nobody can see The Great Chain as it is,
Let me

show you. Will you please
remain here.
Be free here.
Persevere here.
Wander here.
Endear here.
Venture here.
Care here.
Revel here.
Rest here.
Recover here.
Feel here.
End again.
You finish where you started

and the links connect again.
