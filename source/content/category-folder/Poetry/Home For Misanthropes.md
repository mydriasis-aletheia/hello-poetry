Somebody judge me,
Somebody love me,
I'd beg of you
to find some-
-thing
worthwhile within me
but I've been searching for years
and this accursed, most unfounded depression
reappears.
I don't run from it anymore, I confront it now.
It is still there, for some unknown reason; I cope.
I just
don't know
why me. Is this what life really is? Is everybody like this?
I hope it's only me. I wouldn't wish such a thing upon my family.
I don't deserve this, let me explain the extent of what I mean by that,
I am ashamed. I've shelter, food, friends, internet and I'm in uni.
I have no right to suffer (in comparison to the misfortune of others) yet
I continue to write so dramatically. So egocentric is he? Probably! He
is sorry. This is all he has, all
I have, to offer;
I realize what it says about me
which is why I began this poem by imploring you to judge me.

These poems are my only means of coping,
Poetry, among other things, helped save my life.
So thank you for putting up with me (my dear HP). I know at times
I can be difficult. I'd like to think there're times
when I'm worth it (I honestly don't know though)
without being presumptuous or realistic.

Irregardless,
We can all seek some measure of solace
in poetry.

(Somebody say
I'm worth more
than dust.)
