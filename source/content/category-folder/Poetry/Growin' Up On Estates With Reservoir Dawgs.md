Thirsty bunch of dogs
baying at the stars,
Let loose and Come Find Yourself 
down by the reservoir.

Shadows Of Ourselves
been walking insofar.
Heaven's Gonna Burn Your Eyes 
down by the reservoir. 

You know what they say,
Every dawg has its day.
We'll Smoke 'Em
down by the reservoir.


(Schmoove.)
