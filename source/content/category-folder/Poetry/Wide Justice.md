Living with this,
Coping with it: I know now these loops 
are part of me, I know now these feelings 
are permanent; their sways everlasting.

The ebb and flow
of a mind which knows it is in flux
yet also belongs to that unchanging one 
whose breath animates us.

I fall into unconscious shuddering
with desperation and mute wonder
and hidden hopes and silent screams

I recognize what's become fixed within me,
In its grip, I just wish I could cry. No progress,
Bring all that trauma back. The Apotheon is calling

and I'm stone cold but I don't want to sober. I try so hard
but I got all this poison
and don't want to share.
Losing all the time on earth
to the gods of the underworld.
I turn around and see Orpheus 
following me.

(He vanishes.)
