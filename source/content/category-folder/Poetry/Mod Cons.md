'All the mod cons' they say, as if a few appliances 
make up for the studio flat which seems like a cage 
or the extortionate rent that is so unaffordable alone
even for me on my loathsome tech salary
which is causing this problem.
Hundreds of applications, responses,
Tens of viewings, paperwork and follow-ons;
Despite giving them every document and reference,
Every virtue and quality desirable in a prospective tenant,
Even a healthy bank balance,
I am not earning enough
for a decent place 
on my own
with any of the trappings 
that I could work from home
and live out a healthy, dignified
existence in this,
Our capital city.


(All the modern conveniences
with none of the comforts' past.)
#housing crisis
