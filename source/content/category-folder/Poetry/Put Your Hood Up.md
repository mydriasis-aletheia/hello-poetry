Listening to BoC while skimming the dark web,
I forgot how much time I spent here, perusing 
the boards, the forums, running the shadows

and passing dark corners. Crime and liberty
coexist here. Freedom is relative, and many-faced.
Suffering is apparent. It's all very strange.

For those in society who have been displaced,
For whom no bell tolls,
For ware no refuge is safe.

Hackers.
Dealers.
Journalists.

Dissidents.
Whistleblowers.
Anarchists.

Put your hood up.
