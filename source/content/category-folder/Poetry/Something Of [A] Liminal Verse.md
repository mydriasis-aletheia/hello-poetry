To live and love and lie and loath
and feel the pain of spiritual growth.

What consoles you?
Keep [it] going.

Regardless of whether I have a soul
I am content to be.

This me,
Myself and I
are no match
for be and being
from such great height;
I am an odd flux (in a strange loop).

(At least I am human,
Part-human anyway;
Lost in translation.)
