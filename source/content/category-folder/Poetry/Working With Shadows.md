Struggle to attain such peace,
The heavens open,
Smile in the soaking rain.

Our practices must be underscored
by a narrative they can be subsumed by
if they are to elicit change

in the person, if they are to fashion personality
or craft personae. "But I am straitened between two:

Having a desire to be dissolved
and to be with Christ, a thing by far the better.

But to abide still in the flesh, is needful for you".
*[Pulvis et] umbra sumus*.
(We are [dust and] shadow.)


(They cannot be rid of the dark
so they lean into it)
