Nevermind my unjust maudlin,
It's been a while since I've been
back here.
I don't know anymore has been said before
so nobody
dare call me out
or so break my word.
Tell me we don't know anything about them,
Or anyone.
This one? Son of a gun! I caught him
out on the plains, burying someone.

Gotta be honest now, just for a sec:
I want to feel again;
It's been a long haul
and I can barely remember anything.
I’m sure we had it all.
Might as well
enjoy the fall.
