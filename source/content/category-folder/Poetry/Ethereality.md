He walks for me now, out in the street, under the bough.
Definity; the feeling it, to know.
Leave us, there’s only one thing left for me. I miss...

What happened to that quality, the character of life
that enticed me?

Surreality broke my mind,
Ethereality left it behind.
Apotheosis deified,
Entheos sublimed.
The Empathion felt,
The Psychedelion knew.
Everything I did, I thought

was true. Maybe it is, according to point of view.
Take me with you.

I'm lost, alone, in a forest, a room.
In this perfect darkness I can see you.
Here I feel something true, hold my hand
and let us renew. Let’s take leave
of this plane of existence, together
we’ll surpass humanity, transcend the mind.
We’ll warp time, alter space, the divine;
Sublime. Ethereality is what I've

been searching for.

(Missing you.)
