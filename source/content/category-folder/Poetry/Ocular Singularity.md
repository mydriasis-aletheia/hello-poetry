Seeing through 2C-Eye:
He saw life flash before his eyes
so he banked left and took to the skies.
Tripping the broadcast, let the pulse kick,
Can’t stop the signal baby, so listen in.

Looking a lone wolf in the I, teardrop crystalline;
Glimmering with mysticism,
Shivering and high.
(The oculus collapsed.)
