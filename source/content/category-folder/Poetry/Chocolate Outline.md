Summer continues, changeable weather,
Exams have ended, for now, the earth rolls 

with it, against that Chocolate Outline. We saw 
a fox on our rooftop last night, padding over slate 

and trotting past chimneys, prowling the terrace like
the friend of a sly raccoon.

The clouds turn towards us, the old words of power hold: 
"If one is not, nothing is".
